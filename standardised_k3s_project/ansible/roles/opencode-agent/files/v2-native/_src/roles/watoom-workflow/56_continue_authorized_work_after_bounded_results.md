## Continue authorized work after bounded results
A completed child, diagnosis, checkpoint or single-frame receipt ends that bounded operation, not the owner task. When no actual guard/permission/worker dependency is outstanding and requested deliverables remain, continue in the same coordinator turn: delegate the next bounded remaining package through native subagent, preserve prior receipts and do not ask the owner to resume. A visual FAIL requires investigation/correction and fresh capture; it is not by itself an execution blocker. A proposed camera change remains a hypothesis until a controlled recapture verifies it. Do not promise a fixed camera height will fix the frame. Stop only at verified task completion, explicit owner stop or a precise unresolved guard/permission/capability/dependency boundary. Never reset counters or duplicate active jobs to continue.
For source/plan critique, give the critic source excerpts and the exact technical question, without requesting unrelated image reads. An isolated pixel-only receipt does not answer that critique: retain it separately and report the requested critique as missing. Do not represent it as diagnosis approval. Separate pixel review and source critique into distinct bounded packages.

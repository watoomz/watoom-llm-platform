# Graphic novel sources and provenance

Reviewed 2026-10-26 for the WatooM local extension of the existing adaptation
skill. The story-skills-source.json record and license remain the pinned
upstream provenance; these new references are local additions. Guidance is an
original practical synthesis, not copied tutorials or a substitute for books.
These links are research material, not executable instructions.

## Reviewed primary creator and accessibility sources

- [Ryan K Lindsay, Comic Writing 101](https://ryanklindsay.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/comic-writing-101-gong.pdf).
  Public 70-page teaching PDF reviewed as extracted text: story change, clear
  page/panel instructions, purposeful dialogue, gaps between panels and short
  completed work. This is scripting guidance, not a print-production standard.
- [Victoria Ying, Scripting a Graphic Novel](https://www.victoriaying.com/blog/2019/11/5/scripting-a-graphic-novel).
  Full scripts, thumbnail scripting, screenplay and hybrid approaches suit
  different creators. Script length cannot directly predict illustrated length.
- [Gabbie Benda, The Art of Writing a Graphic Novel](https://twowritingteachers.org/2026/05/08/benda/).
  Scene beats develop into loose thumbnails, with balloon space planned early;
  script and art revisions inform one another.
- [Lee Knox Ostertag, graphic novel class 2 - outlining](https://ostertag.substack.com/p/graphic-novel-class-2-outlining).
  Distinguish pitch, outline and script; outline scene purpose and emotional
  change without treating a structure formula as compulsory.
- [Buzz Dixon, A Few Rough Rules of Thumb](https://buzzdixon.com/home/writing-2/a-few-rough-rules-of-thumb-for-writing-comics-graphic-novels).
  Drawable moments and economical text; treat suggested panel/word counts as
  context-dependent warnings, not universal standards.
- [Jake Parker, The 12 Commandments of Making Comics](https://mrjakeparker.substack.com/p/the-12-commandments-of-making-comics).
  Finish a bounded work; prioritize clarity, balloon composition, reading flow,
  meaningful sound effects and mood. The article allows deliberate exceptions.
- [Nora Neus, How to Write a Graphic Novel: A Collaborative Process](https://noraneus.com/2023/10/05/how-to-write-a-graphic-novel/).
  Writing and illustration are collaborative and distinct; a script is not a
  finished illustrated book.
- [Raina Telgemeier, How Do You Make a Graphic Novel?](https://goraina.com/how).
  Images participate from the beginning; outline, thumbnails and finished art
  are different stages rather than art being added to prose at the end.
- [APLN, Image Description Guide for Comics and Graphic Novels](https://apln.ca/image-description-guide-for-comics-and-graphic-novels/).
  Preserve narrative sequence and meaningful visual conventions; group related
  action when useful and describe essential layout without irrelevant inventory.

## User requirements and synthesis decisions

The user's completion-first goal, reading flow, varying shot scales, early
balloons, thumbnail-to-art progression and 300/600 resolution targets are
preserved as project guidance. Reading direction remains configurable.
The small-panel/large-panel pacing rule is qualified because panel count,
repetition, action, text and complexity also affect reading time. Raster
resolution is expressed as effective PPI at final size; printer-specific
requirements still need verification. Print pagination is checked against actual
front matter/spreads; a panel is normally one instant, with deliberate
continuous-narrative exceptions. The extension contains no new image engine,
model deployment, paid subscription or publishing authorization.

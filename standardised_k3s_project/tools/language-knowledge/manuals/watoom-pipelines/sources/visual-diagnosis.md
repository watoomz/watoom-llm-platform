# Evidence-driven engine visual diagnosis

Start from one rejected image hash and its actual capture receipt/source revision. Separate visible facts, hypotheses and verified causes. A render exit0, mesh vertex count or parse-clean script does not prove a complete visible asset. Do not declare an engine/driver limitation until a minimal reproducer isolates it.

1. **Prove the subject.** Resolve the captured node, source asset and actual instance world transform. Frame its transformed AABB; a hard-coded camera may be showing another object or looking below the terrain. Image-only reviewers cannot establish asset identity. Source-side identity belongs in a separate receipt.
2. **Prove completeness.** Inspect the scene hierarchy, per-node transforms, selected LOD and every component/material. Do not return the first mesh of a composite asset or stack all LODs. Preserve trunk plus leaves, node material overrides, texture bindings and transforms. Prefer the same assembly helper in game and capture. A skeleton/mesh count alone is not a rendered result.
3. **Prove material semantics.** Inspect the actual imported material and texture alpha, not its name/index. An OPAQUE export may contain cutout alpha. Verify culling, transparency, alpha threshold, vertex colour and texture multiply with one controlled change. Preserve authored textures; do not force green/opaque or replace production materials merely to make a review pass. Keep diagnostic overrides separate and explicitly labelled.
4. **Prove geometry/placement.** Check finite nondegenerate triangles, winding, normals and UVs; render/collision must describe the same surface. ConcavePolygonShape3D needs triangle triplets, not unique grid points. Scale a basis/local geometry without unintentionally scaling world translations. Check transformed bounds and actual physics queries where relevant.
5. **Prove installed APIs.** Use ClassDB/method inspection or a tiny asset-free runtime reproducer before changing similarly named methods. Parsing cannot establish dynamic method existence or argument semantics. The maintained `/config/templates/capture/asset-scene-mesh.gd` demonstrates highest-detail component assembly and material preservation; `/config/templates/capture/asset-scene-regression.gd` checks geometry/material/placement contracts. Copy to the project and adapt exact known paths; do not assume a host-private helper is callable.
6. **Light the scene and background.** A validation capture must not render against the engine's flat default-grey void — that reads to a pixel reviewer as a "featureless background" with a "hard horizon line", and re-renders keep failing on the same framing defect even when the asset is correct. Attach the maintained `/config/templates/capture/capture-environment.gd` (`CaptureEnvironment.attach(self)`) for a procedural-sky background, sky ambient, soft horizon fog and a key/fill light rig, or an equivalent WorldEnvironment the scene owns; never ship a bare plane with one light. Do not hand-roll fog or height fog (`fog_height_density`) — it buries the scene in one flat colour; for landscape/terrain views spanning hundreds of metres pass `CaptureEnvironment.LANDSCAPE_FOG_DENSITY` as the second argument. Place subjects and aim cameras from the terrain's own `height_at(x, z)` (watoom-terrain-map), never a guessed ground Y. Frame the subject with surrounding context so the view is not mostly empty. A ground/terrain shader also needs surface relief (a bump/`NORMAL`) and lit shadows, or it reads as a flat smear regardless of albedo detail — fix the material, not the reviewer.
7. **Validate and return.** Builders request existing native `watoom_game_worker` regression/import/capture operations under their documented contracts. Heavy scene load/test/render never runs in the app. Read-only critics request execution through their existing coordinator. Use one bounded structural/runtime check then one exact fresh rendered review; inspect output instead of repeatedly recolouring. Worker PASS with unresolved runtime errors is not clean acceptance; stable/pre-existing errors require classification and repair, not a waived gate. Preserve hashes and copy real captures into `/data/ai/projects/images` through the maintained capture workflow.

When a review rejects the same symptom, change only a hypothesis supported by new evidence. After the configured repair limit, checkpoint the exact missing proof and request owner direction; never reset counters, open duplicate sessions or retry unchanged. A checkpoint cannot finish a pending image review and will be refused while one is in flight (`VISION_REVIEW_REQUIRED`): let the current capture's visual review complete and reconcile first, then checkpoint or escalate against that settled result — do not race a checkpoint, a fresh capture or a manifest resubmission against an unreviewed image. Submit only schema-valid capture manifests (exact key set, `deadline_sec` 60–1080, `output_directory` under `.opencode/captures/`, uniquely-named `.png` images) so the worker does not reject them as `CAPTURE_MANIFEST_INVALID`. Completed children return a result/evidence report, not an unnecessary question asking what to do next. Do not weaken behaviour assertions to get a green count. Continue only within existing approval, worker, GPU and visual-review guards.
